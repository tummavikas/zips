# Electric Vehicle Guide

## Benefits of Electric Vehicles

- Low fuel expenses
- Lower maintenance costs
- Smooth driving experience
- Reduced environmental impact

---

## Important Factors

### Vehicle Range

Choose an EV with range exceeding daily travel requirements.

### Charging Infrastructure

Check:

- Home charging availability
- Workplace charging options
- Public charging stations

### Battery Warranty

Most manufacturers provide:

- 6 to 8 years battery warranty

---

## Ideal EV Buyers

Electric vehicles are ideal for:

- Urban commuters
- Daily office travel
- Families with charging access
