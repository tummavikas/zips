# Vehicle Buying Guide

## Hatchbacks

Advantages:

- Affordable purchase cost
- Better fuel economy
- Easy parking

Suitable For:

- Students
- First-time buyers
- Small families

---

## Sedans

Advantages:

- Comfortable ride quality
- Large boot space
- Better highway stability

Suitable For:

- Professionals
- Long-distance commuters

---

## SUVs

Advantages:

- Higher driving position
- Better ground clearance
- Spacious interiors

Suitable For:

- Families
- Mixed city and rural usage

---

## MPVs

Advantages:

- High seating capacity
- Family-friendly design

Suitable For:

- Joint families
- Commercial transport

---

## Electric Vehicles

Advantages:

- Low operating costs
- Reduced maintenance
- Environment friendly

Best For:

- Daily city driving
- Urban commuting
