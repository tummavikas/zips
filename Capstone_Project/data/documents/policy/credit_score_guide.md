# Credit Score Guide

## Excellent Credit

Range: 800 - 900

Characteristics:

- High approval probability
- Better interest rate offers
- Faster loan processing

---

## Good Credit

Range: 750 - 799

Characteristics:

- Strong approval chances
- Competitive interest rates

---

## Fair Credit

Range: 650 - 749

Characteristics:

- May require additional review
- Moderate approval probability

---

## Poor Credit

Range: Below 650

Characteristics:

- Increased lending risk
- Higher rejection probability

---

## Ways to Improve Credit Score

- Pay EMIs on time
- Maintain low credit utilization
- Avoid frequent loan applications
- Monitor credit reports regularly
