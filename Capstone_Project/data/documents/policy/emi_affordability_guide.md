# EMI Affordability Guide

## What is Affordability?

Affordability measures the ability of an applicant to repay a loan without financial stress.

---

## What is FOIR?

FOIR stands for Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio.

Formula:

FOIR = (Existing EMIs + Proposed EMI) ÷ Monthly Income

---

## Recommended FOIR Range

Excellent: Below 35%

Good: 35% to 45%

Acceptable: Up to 50%

High Risk: Above 50%

---

## Improving Affordability

Applicants can improve affordability by:

- Increasing down payment
- Choosing longer tenure
- Reducing existing debt
- Selecting lower-priced vehicles
