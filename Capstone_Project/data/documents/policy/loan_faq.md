# Vehicle Loan Frequently Asked Questions

## What affects my vehicle loan approval?

Vehicle loan approval depends on:

- Credit score
- Monthly income
- Employment stability
- Existing EMI obligations
- Requested loan amount
- Down payment amount
- Age of applicant

---

## What credit score is considered good?

A credit score above 750 is generally considered strong.

Scores between 650 and 750 may still qualify subject to policy review.

Scores below 650 are considered higher risk.

---

## How does down payment affect eligibility?

A higher down payment reduces the financed amount and improves approval chances.

Applicants making larger down payments generally qualify for better loan terms.

---

## How is EMI calculated?

EMI depends on:

- Loan amount
- Interest rate
- Loan tenure

Longer tenure reduces monthly EMI but increases total interest paid.

---

## Can I prepay my vehicle loan?

Prepayment is generally allowed, subject to lender-specific terms and charges.

---

## What documents are required?

Typical documents include:

- Identity Proof
- Address Proof
- Income Proof
- Bank Statements
- Vehicle Quotation
