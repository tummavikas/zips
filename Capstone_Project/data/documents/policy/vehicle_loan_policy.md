# Example Vehicle Loan Policy

**Document ID:** VL-POLICY-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Synthetic educational policy; not a real lender's policy.

## 1. Minimum eligibility

- Applicant must be at least 21 years old and no older than 65 at the end of the loan.
- Minimum gross monthly income is ₹25,000.
- Minimum credit score is 650.
- Salaried applicants need at least 1 year of employment experience. Self-employed applicants need at least 2 years of business experience.
- The applicant must provide identity, address, income, and vehicle documents before disbursement.

## 2. Repayment capacity

- Total monthly debt obligations after the proposed vehicle-loan EMI must not exceed 50% of gross monthly income (FOIR limit).
- Existing monthly EMIs are included in total obligations.
- For this demonstration, the indicative annual interest rate is 10.5% and the maximum tenure is 84 months.
- The lender may sanction less than the requested amount when repayment capacity is lower.

## 3. Loan-to-value and amount

- The loan may finance no more than 90% of the vehicle's on-road price (LTV limit).
- The sanctioned loan must not exceed the amount requested by the applicant.
- The final eligible principal is the lowest of the requested principal, repayment-capacity principal, and applicable LTV principal.
- The EMI estimate is indicative; fees, insurance, taxes, and lender-specific pricing may change the final offer.

## 4. Decision and exceptions

- An application that fails a minimum eligibility requirement is rejected.
- A model score is a decision-support signal and does not override the eligibility, repayment-capacity, or LTV requirements in this policy.
- No policy exception is implemented in this educational application. Refer cases requiring an exception to a human loan officer.

## 5. Important limitation

This synthetic policy and the synthetic training dataset exist only for a software capstone demonstration. They are not financial advice, a credit offer, or a substitute for a regulated lender's underwriting process.
